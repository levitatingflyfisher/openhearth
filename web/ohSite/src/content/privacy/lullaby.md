Lullaby stores every entry you make — feedings, sleeps, diapers,
milestones — on your device only. There is no account, no server
call, and no analytics.

## What Lullaby collects

- Nothing. The app has no network permissions for core features.
- When you export data, it is written to a file you control.

## What Lullaby sends

- Nothing.

## Crash reports

- Disabled by default. Off unless you explicitly opt in.
