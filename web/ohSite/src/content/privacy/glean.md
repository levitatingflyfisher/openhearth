Glean's data is a folder of plain-text journal files on your device. Nothing ever
leaves unless you explicitly export or sync it yourself.

## What Glean collects

- Your transactions, categories, budgets, and notes.
- All stored as plain-text hledger journals in a folder you control.

## What Glean sends

- Nothing.
