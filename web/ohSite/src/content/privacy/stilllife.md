StillLife stores your entire home inventory — photos, items, rooms, values —
on your device only. There is no account, no server call, and no analytics.

## What StillLife collects

- Nothing leaves your device. All inventory data is stored in local SQLite.
- Exported PDFs are written to a file you control — never uploaded.

## What StillLife sends

- Nothing.

## Crash reports

- Disabled by default. Off unless you explicitly opt in.
