Porch uses a blind-relay server that cannot read who is pinging
whom. Presence tokens are rotated on a TTL and are not linked to
identity in the server's logs.

## What Porch collects

- Your friend list, stored encrypted on your device.
- An anonymous device token used for presence routing.

## What Porch sends

- Rotating presence tokens to the relay server.
- No messages, no user identifiers, no analytics.
