Sundial tracks outdoor-time entries on your device. If you enable
family sync, entries are end-to-end encrypted before leaving your
device — the server stores ciphertext only.

## What Sundial collects

- Timer entries (start, stop, optional label) — local only by default.
- If you enable sync: an anonymous account UUID and encrypted blobs.

## What Sundial sends

- Nothing in ghost mode.
- In sync mode: encrypted data only. No plaintext, no telemetry.
