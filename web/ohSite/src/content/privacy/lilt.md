Lilt stores your name rankings on your device. Comparing with a
partner uses an opt-in end-to-end encrypted pairing — nothing is
ever exposed in plaintext.

## What Lilt collects

- Your local ranking list and comparison history.
- When paired: encrypted blobs shared between paired devices.

## What Lilt sends

- Nothing in solo mode.
- In paired mode: encrypted data only.
